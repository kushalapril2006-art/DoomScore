Doomscore Android Privacy Policy

Provided by the Doomscore team. Proposed contact, taken from the supplied iOS repository: suyashsinghdtg@gmail.com. The team must confirm this contact before publication.

Automatic counting and your consent

When you agree and enable Android Accessibility, Doomscore reads visible interface text, descriptions and control IDs from the reel apps you select, including when Doomscore is in the background. It uses that information to identify reel changes, visible ad labels and recent repeat views. Other apps are ignored. You can pause in Doomscore or turn off its service in Android Accessibility at any time.

What stays on your phone

Doomscore stores daily, hourly and per-app counts, viewing time, recognized-ad/rewatch totals, UTC daily league totals, preferences and salted one-way hashes for recent reels. Hashes support a five-minute recognition window; expired entries are removed during use or when you next open the app. Captions and visible labels are processed temporarily, are not saved, and are never uploaded. The default test release creates no server accounts and uploads no counting data. Profile drafts are encrypted on this phone and do not reserve a global username.

Optional global Doom League

If the online league is enabled, choosing a username and agreeing to join creates an anonymous Supabase identity without requiring an email or password. Current-month UTC daily reel totals sync over HTTPS. Supabase receives connection information including your IP address. Your chosen username, avatar, optional self-reported Instagram username and monthly reel total are public. The board displays the top 50, your exact rank through 200, and a top percentage below that. Previous-month top-three profiles are highlighted on UTC days 1–7. An Instagram link is not verification of account ownership.

Private credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore. A username is not a password or account-recovery method. Uninstalling can lose access to an anonymous identity. Hide your profile or remove its Instagram link in League → Edit. Reports and block lists are private; moderators can review reports. A signup security check may send connection/browser information to Cloudflare Turnstile. Local counting and browsing do not require joining.

No screenshots or screen recordings are taken. Doomscore does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location. It includes no advertising or analytics SDK, does not sell your data, and does not use your information for advertising.

Security

Local data is stored in the Android application sandbox. App backups are disabled. Android's device-storage protection depends on your phone and security settings. The app does not claim to protect data on a compromised or rooted device.

Achievement badges

The Brainrot Trophy Cabinet retains a bounded set of salted reel hashes to identify unique qualified views: up to 10,000 lifetime hashes and 500 daily hashes. Lifetime hashes are removed after the final lifetime milestone is earned. The daily set resets with the calendar day and is removed/stops accumulating after the 500-in-a-day milestone is earned. Aggregate badge progress and unlock dates remain until you delete local counting history. These hashes do not sync. Battle and global-rank trophies use private server-verified results tied to your anonymous identity; their cache is encrypted on the phone and online records are deleted with that identity.

Retention and deletion

Aggregate history stays on this installation until you delete local counting history in Doomscore Settings or uninstall the app. Deleting local history removes local counts and recent hashes but does not erase previously synced totals. Settings remain unless you clear Android app data or uninstall. League → Edit → Delete identity removes online profiles, scores, podium entries, reports and blocks belonging to that identity; local counts remain. Failed server deletion can be retried. When the online league is disabled, Delete identity removes only the local profile draft.

Optional live counter displays

The Goob island draws a small draggable counter over selected reel feeds using the Accessibility permission you enabled. It does not capture the screen. Optional island-app notifications share today's reel count, rank and source app with Android; notification-listener apps you authorize may read them. The notification is silent and hides score details on the lock screen by default, subject to your Android settings. Both displays stop when you leave a detected feed or pause counting. Native phone islands and third-party notification hosts control their own rendering and compatibility.

Sharing and external links

Sharing a recap opens Android's share chooser. Only the generated image and text are supplied to the receiving app you choose; that app's privacy practices apply. Opening a policy or support link uses the browser/mail app on your phone. No recap is sent automatically.

Accuracy and future changes

Recognition depends on the interface information other apps expose. Changes in app versions/language, identical metadata and unlabelled ads can affect counts. Any future account, cloud-sync, CAPTCHA, analytics or advertising functionality requires an updated policy and disclosures before activation.
