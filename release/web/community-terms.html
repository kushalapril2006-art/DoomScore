Doom League Community Terms

The Doom League is a playful monthly comparison of reel counts. You can use local counting without joining. Joining makes your chosen username, avatar, monthly score and optional Instagram handle public. The leaderboard is not a health recommendation or an invitation to exceed your personal limits.

Respect other people

Choose a username that does not impersonate another person. Do not use hateful, threatening, sexually explicit or harassing names. Share only your own Instagram username or a handle you are authorized to represent. Instagram links are self-reported and are not verified by Doomscore.

Keep scores honest

Do not modify the app to fabricate counts, automate watching to inflate a score, attack the service or create identities to manipulate rankings. Server validation may clamp implausible totals. Accounts may be hidden or suspended by moderators for abuse; reports do not automatically alter scores or suspend people.

Monthly seasons

Global seasons follow UTC calendar months. Scores sync while connected. Counts that remain offline across the month boundary may not reach the closed season. The top three of a closed month are highlighted during the next month's first seven UTC days. Ties use a consistent internal ordering. Detection depends on what other apps expose and may sometimes miss or misclassify reels. No prizes are promised by these terms.

Your identity and controls

Joining uses a private anonymous credential, without an email or password. Your username alone cannot recover your identity after uninstalling or losing credentials. You can hide your profile, edit or remove your Instagram link, block or report a profile, and delete your online identity. Deleting local history does not delete already synced scores. Read the separately published Android privacy policy for details.

Reports and moderation

Use the profile menu to report impersonation, offensive content or spam. The publisher's moderators review the private report queue and may restrict profiles. Blocked profiles disappear from your view without changing other people's rankings. Contact the publisher through the support details supplied in the app for help or to contest a moderation decision.
