## 1.6.8 chart and reliability update

Hold or tap an hour/day/month bar to reveal its exact period and reel count; hold then drag to compare adjacent periods. Full-width hit slots include zero counts. Keyboard and accessible actions use the same buckets. Year mode shows the last 12 calendar months; month mode browses previous calendar months and blocks future navigation. More-stats source bars and reel-based trophy progress also expose count readouts.

Neutral navy-charcoal dark surfaces and warm light surfaces keep the Scoreboard hierarchy. Green remains the Today/Goob brand accent; cyan marks hourly/selected bars, violet marks Stats/history, gold marks League/first place and pink marks Wrapped. The bundled two-font system and brief motion are preserved.

The Trophy Cabinet labels counted views and lifetime unique separately. These quantities can legitimately differ because of older repeats and the date unique tracking began. Accepted views now update score, hourly/UTC totals and unique progress atomically, with one revision. History snapshots read related ranges together. Counter scanning/writes move off the main thread, stale bindings lose authority after disconnect, and app resume wakes a bound poller. An unbound service gets an explicit reconnect action rather than an indefinite connecting label. See [counter findings](ACCESSIBILITY-RELIABILITY.md).

The updated source passes 124 JVM checks, and the debug app/Android-check APK compile. The signed private candidate passes APK/signature/permission/ZIP/16 KB alignment checks; lint reports zero errors and 75 warnings. No dependency or manifest permission was added.

A temporary native checker kept accessibility services enabled, queried only DoomScore's hierarchy, and exercised real touches. ## Earlier Scoreboard implementation

Version 1.6.6 applies the selected Scoreboard design: warm off-white or system dark surfaces, a single green accent, score-left/Goob-right hierarchy and flat source totals. It simplifies Today to Goob, the daily score, a session count, source totals and the hourly chart. The hero and chart are no longer boxed cards. Rank coaching and next-up labels are removed. Setup appears as a compact action only when needed. Calendar-month totals, streaks, personal bests, skipped ads and rewatches live in Stats, alongside dedicated Trophy Cabinet and Wrapped destinations. Detailed statistics and source breakdowns sit behind More stats to keep the default page uncluttered.

League opens with a compact month/UTC-season label and the standings. The top three occupy a podium; subsequent places use numbered rows and aligned reel totals. Your position and season score stay outside the scrolling list, above navigation. Account opens a separate sheet containing Google sign-in/verification, public-profile editing and account notices. Previous-month winners and their optional Instagram links appear only during UTC days 1–7 for the current season. Public profiles retain reporting and blocking, including podium profiles. Pagination is unchanged.

Space Grotesk is the bold display family for totals and titles; DM Sans handles usernames, settings and descriptions. Both are bundled for offline use. Navigation uses outline icons instead of emoji. Totals shrink to available width; large text stacks the Today hero. Settings stays reachable in the header. Long usernames wrap or truncate within their column. Podium Instagram links use a short accessible label; profile menus are available by tapping another player's row or podium position. Large-text layouts replace the three-column podium with numbered rows and place each reel total below its username.

Motion uses short score fades, chart updates, gentle selected-tab feedback and directional page transitions around 200 ms. Native Compose timing respects the device animator scale. Goob is the original colour-changing blob, with a gentle idle contour and occasional blink. Tapping Today's Goob gives one brief squish/wink/smile and light haptic response; repeated taps do not stack reactions or change counts. The contour uses shared trigonometric tables, caller-owned arrays and remembered paths, with draw-only state updates capped at 30 per second. The idle loop and tap animation stop when the app leaves the foreground, system animation is disabled or battery saver is enabled. Reduced motion retains a short static wink. League refresh polling also stops in the background. A score milestone label appears for 1.4 seconds. Returning to a tab does not replay old milestones.

Tab scroll positions and the Stats period survive navigation/rotation. Back returns from a primary tab to Today while sheets retain their own dismissal. Recap Close and Share stay visible while its content scrolls. Counter setup opens expanded; its Done action stays fixed in the header while settings scroll. Onboarding Start/Explore/Privacy stay visible while the introduction scrolls. Username and Instagram errors appear alongside their fields; server errors stay beside Save. Counters/privacy/deletion stay available without wellness goals.

History loads with two indexed range queries instead of two per day (730 reads for a 365-day view). The monthly Stats total uses one aggregate query; Today loads only its daily snapshot. Database snapshots load on a background dispatcher; revisions are sampled for visible score refreshes and collected only while the UI is active. Real counter events are not sampled, discarded or slowed by this UI sampling. Existing counter, aggregation and trophy rules are unchanged.

Loading failures offer retry, and refreshes keep the previous good snapshot. Compose Foundation, Layout and Animation are explicitly aligned with UI 1.9.2, so debug tooling cannot silently promote runtime-only versions and leave calls to removed experimental FlowRow overloads. The personal position is keyed by its rank, score and profile to invalidate the constrained subcomposition when standings change.

Version 1.6.6 passed 108 JVM unit checks, three instrumented checks (tap reaction including reduced motion, live pinned-rank changes and trophy navigation), 18 primary UI acceptance checks, and 13 offline league/profile checks on an isolated API 36.1 Android emulator. Four additional responsive scenarios verified Today, Settings, League, Account and Stats at 320dp width, in both light/dark mode and normal/200% text size. The dependency scan checked 257 exact Maven/npm versions against OSV on 10 October 2026, with no known advisories found. This does not prove absence of vulnerabilities. Physical-phone acceptance and future Instagram/YouTube accessibility layouts remain separate; no universal accuracy or specific FPS claim is made.

The optimized, non-debuggable 1.6.6 installation candidate passed lint with zero errors (74 warnings), restricted manifest/permission checks, existing-key signature verification, ZIP integrity and 16 KB native alignment. Public-launch gates and live-backend acceptance remain unchanged.
