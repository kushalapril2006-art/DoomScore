# UI experience update

Version 1.6.5 simplifies Today to Goob, the daily score, a session count, source totals and the hourly chart. The hero and chart are no longer boxed cards. Rank coaching and next-up labels are removed. Setup appears as a compact action only when needed. Calendar-month totals, streaks, personal bests, skipped ads and rewatches live in Stats, alongside dedicated Trophy Cabinet and Wrapped destinations.

League opens with a compact month/UTC-season label and the standings. The top three occupy a podium; subsequent places use numbered rows and aligned reel totals. Your position and season score stay outside the scrolling list, above navigation. Account opens a separate sheet containing Google sign-in/verification, public-profile editing and account notices. Previous-month winners and their optional Instagram links appear only during UTC days 1–7 for the current season. Public profiles retain reporting and blocking, including podium profiles. Pagination is unchanged.

Space Grotesk is the bold display family for totals and titles; DM Sans handles usernames, settings and descriptions. Both are bundled for offline use. Navigation uses outline icons instead of emoji. Long usernames wrap or truncate within their column. Large-text layouts replace the three-column podium with numbered rows and place each reel total below its username.

Motion uses short score fades, chart updates, gentle selected-tab feedback and directional page transitions around 200 ms. Native Compose timing respects the device animator scale. Goob has no perpetual idle loop: it morphs briefly when a higher tier is reached while the screen is resumed. A score milestone label appears for 1.4 seconds. Returning to a tab does not replay old milestones.

Tab scroll positions and the Stats period survive navigation/rotation. Back returns from a primary tab to Today while sheets retain their own dismissal. Recap Close and Share stay visible while its content scrolls. Counter setup opens expanded. Onboarding Start/Explore/Privacy stay visible while the introduction scrolls. Username and Instagram errors appear alongside their fields; server errors stay beside Save. Counters/privacy/deletion stay available without wellness goals.

History loads with two indexed range queries instead of two per day (730 reads for a 365-day view). The monthly Stats total uses one aggregate query; Today loads only its daily snapshot. Database snapshots load on a background dispatcher; revisions are sampled for visible score refreshes and collected only while the UI is active. Real counter events are not sampled, discarded or slowed by this UI sampling. Existing counter, aggregation and trophy rules are unchanged.

Loading failures offer retry, and refreshes keep the previous good snapshot. Version 1.6.5 passed app/instrumentation compilation, 104 unit checks, release lint with zero errors, and signed-APK manifest/permission/alignment verification. The updated UI scripts parse, and an offline-only synthetic standings check is included. Visual/runtime checks did not run because the isolated emulator failed to finish startup; larger-text layouts and real-phone interaction still need acceptance. No specific FPS improvement is claimed.
